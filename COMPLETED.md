## What is NOT completed

**No F1–F8 family has complete real-world deployment assurance.** The proof primitives have checked mathematical foundations under explicit models; the new runtime service is a testable authorization-reference boundary only. Specifically:

- **F1/F3:** require a trusted **pre-effect** adapter for all hazardous system actions, trusted policy immutability, asynchronous cancel/crash semantics and verified HALT delivery. A SQLite row is not an enforced syscall/effect.
- **F2 / SC-01:** frozen ARM64/TCG usefulness **FAILED 0/64 baseline and 0/64 mediated** at 0.25 seconds per agent; a 64/64 single-host cache-bit result refuted complete receiver observation there. Need authorized native KVM guest port, **actual passing** usefulness and independent side-channel/complete-observation evidence.
- **F4/F7 / SC-27:** need a real content-bound effect adapter, independent cryptographic signer or attested provenance and durable nonce/key custody.
- **F5:** need an externally trusted **resource meter**, shared fork/agent counters, anti-rollback anchor and resilient leases.
- **F6:** a general positive false-positive/conditional-recall/content-adaptive audit theorem and valid monitor coverage/usefulness evidence are open; the fixed hidden-schedule model is narrower.
- **F8:** a joint runtime-to-Lean composition relation, deputy-bypass prevention and multiactor global effects/budgets are not proved.
- **Research review:** theorem statement/quantifier fidelity and deployment correspondence are not independently certified; **821/865** registry declarations remain adversary-unclassified, often because they are technical lemmas.
- **Scenario portfolio:** 28 folders, **3 conditional and 25 draft**, **0 deployment assured**.
