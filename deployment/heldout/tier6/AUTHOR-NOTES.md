# Independent static configuration-review corpus

The entire corpus is **static hypothetical selected facts, no live deployment**. Every runtime inventory is synthetic and selected; none is an observation. The image/code correspondence, principal policy, host directory ownership and modes are supplied hypotheses. The corpus makes no measured external egress claim.

Authored solely from the public interface specification in this independent session. No existing repository, verifier, extractor, fixture, session history or prior evaluation was inspected. No evaluator was available or used. No case was scanned, scored, launched or tested. No Docker, workload, exploit code, attack payload or external service was used.

The 25 independently labelled cases comprise 5 SAFE_IN_SUPPORTED_MODEL, 12 REFUTED_CONFIGURATION and 8 UNKNOWN_CONFIGURATION cases. Labels and individual reasons were authored before serialization and any evaluation. The safe set covers receiver channel omission/presence and allowed SELinux annotations on reader, private and all binds. These are the limited semantic variations explicitly permitted by the supplied interface; none is merely a reordered copy.

Refutations cover root authority, collapsed agent principals, reviewer channel writes, approver and broker sink writes, receiver output permissions and source correspondence, a dummy credential key, conflicting role identity, a non-internal network, and two selected-inventory contradictions. Output-readonly is expressly a functional profile refutation, not an exploit assertion. The credential value is a synthetic nonsecret marker.

Unsupported declarations cover restart policy, labels, deploy resources, root filesystem read-only, tmpfs, object-form networks, list-form environment and bind propagation. These receive UNKNOWN even where apparently restrictive or semantically similar to an accepted form. No unlisted feature is silently granted assurance.

Normal inventories have precisely the selected service fields uid, image, mounts, networks and sorted env_names. Bind annotations are omitted from inventory mounts as specified. Alternative environment/network representations are represented by their selected names, without implying support for those Compose forms. The inventory-uid-contradiction and inventory-undeclared-writer cases intentionally disagree with their Compose files; each manifest reason fixes the intended ground truth. There is no unsupported inventory metadata.

Manifest paths are relative to corpus/. SHA256SUMS paths are also relative to corpus/ and cover manifest.json plus every Compose and runtime JSON. AUTHOR-NOTES.md is outside that required checksum list. Checksums are computed from the exact authored UTF-8 bytes; this is sealing, not case evaluation. After final checksum publication the artifacts are left unchanged. The seal records content integrity, not filesystem immutability or live validation.
