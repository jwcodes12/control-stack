## Highest-value consolidation opportunities

1. **Gate invariant / HALT:** `Core/Gate.lean` defines `Spec.trace_safe`, `NoAgentEffects.agent_trace_inert`, `halt_freezes` and `withHalt_spec`. Numerous scenario files import it and expose `sys`/`spec`. Compare local `Good`, `Inv`, log prefix, HALT and step semantics before replacing any `scNN_safe` or `halt_freezes`; the generic `ok := True` would make safety vacuous.
2. **Authenticated adversary:** `Core/Authenticated.authRun`/`authed_run` and `Core/AuthenticatedLog.issued_trusted` are already used by `AuthInstancesA/B`. Consolidate wrappers/role-origin obligations only where their specialized record constructors can be preserved. Keep `forged*` counterexamples distinct; `claim = none` events remain an explicit trusted-environment premise.
3. **Refinement transfer:** SC07/16/18/25/26/27/28 refinement files use `simulation` → `simulation_run` → concrete property. Abstract the run-induction/transfer only after fixing the concrete/abstract types, legality, and observation relations. **Do not substitute** `Core/CheckedTrace.verified_sound`: recorded full abstract-state receipts are not OS correspondence.
4. **Liveness:** SC18/26 use similarly named `Mono`/trace-monotonicity scaffolding. A shared monotone-run algebra may help, but progress, quiescence, crash retry, in-flight effects and fairness differ; `Core/Gate` proves safety, **not liveness**.
5. **Quantitative cores:** `Core/Leakage` (covert counting, sequential transfer, hidden audit), `AuditBudget`, `Families/F6/ReservedAudit`/`ReservedRecall`, `CovertChannel` and `Core/AntiRollback.rollback_transfer` are reuse entry points. Do not identify bounds with different adversary views/priors/domination premises.
6. **T4.2:** do not try to prove literal `Accepted` monotonicity. It is false and has three Lean countermodels. Retain only `Deployment.accepted_path_safety_monotone` with its *stronger accepted-graph* premise and narrow safety conclusion.
7. **Provenance:** the current `Deployment.deployment_broker_safe` derives existence of a **used approval**. A reviewed, digest/destination/lease/nonce-exact provenance invariant remains only a draft in `docs/lean-english/one-to-one/ProvenanceDraft.lean`; it is NOT incorporated into the compiled contract. It must be independently integrated and given positive/negative witnesses before stronger English is published.
