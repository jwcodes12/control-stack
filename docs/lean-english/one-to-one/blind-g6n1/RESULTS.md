## Evidence and comparison method

Phase-A hashes in `back/BACK-HASHES.json` were checked against all ten files. Every back file compiled **as written**, with exit code zero. No repairs were necessary: `compile-fixes/<n>.diff` records no change, and `typed/*.lean` is byte-identical to `back/*.lean`. The [typed hashes](typed/HASHES.json) were saved at **2026-10-13T05:59:25.162789+00:00**, before any original declaration was opened. See [blind audit](BLIND-AUDIT.json), [original declaration types](original-statements.txt), and [original source hashes](ORIGINAL-HASHES.json).

Every statement includes `FrozenBack`, transcribing the compiled back definition's RHS. Its `Claim` includes an equality aligning this frozen proposition to its parameterized conclusion, followed by the conclusion comparison. The `Orig` definitions transcribe the original declarations' conclusions, not their proven truth. Claims 1, 2, 3, 8, 9, and 10 have reflexive pointwise iff proofs; changed binder order in claim 2 does not change the hypotheses or conclusion. The safety theorems themselves are never invoked to manufacture equivalence.

For claim 4, `BackRow R g e ↔ OrigRow R g e` holds for **arbitrary** roles, ghost log, and execution record. The reverse direction decomposes the tuple and substitutes its field equalities. `grun_fst` aligns the ghost run's state with the original abstract run; the original injectivity premise is retained. Thus the proof compares the same executed records under the same hash premise.

Claims 5 and 7 are closed concrete examples. Their gate statements therefore include **general bridges over arbitrary logs of length one**, plus independently checked length-one facts for the specified traces, plus the specialized comparison. The proof transfers the assumed existence/membership fact through these bridges. It does not prove both closed safety conclusions and combine their truth. Claim 5 also uses `grun_fst`; claim 7 proves for arbitrary issued traces that an always-identity-free guard equals the plain run. Without the singleton constraint, existence/membership would be weaker than the original exact-list conclusions.

For claim 6, Back explicitly identifies a prefix, the correctly issued audit/approval, a suffix, and the step appending the exact record. Orig only identifies an issued operation occurring in the trace with matching id/verdict/actor/role. The forward proof forgets the extra prefix and append evidence. The reverse proof preserves Orig's assumed `Good` and issuer/role evidence, and uses a generic fold induction to locate the step that appended each final record. The authenticated transition's case analysis proves that every new verdict/approval is appended by the matching authenticated operation. This gives a uniform constructive bridge for each trace, rather than an iff between two independently proved universal safety theorems. No restriction that all issuers lie in `U` is introduced.

Witnesses are non-degenerate: claim 1 executes an irreversible operation after an audit, approval, and crash/re-check; claim 2 starts from a real expiry and records a late PASS while execution stays blocked; claim 3 freezes an already nonempty execution log; claims 4 and 9 instantiate injectivity with the identity hash and produce actual audited effects; claim 5 executes the unaudited amended content; claim 6 mixes a forged audit with genuinely issued audit/approval and executes irreversible content; claim 7 exercises both authentication outcomes; claim 8 executes reversible and irreversible content across crash/restart; claim 10 delivers an approved payment with a duplicate arrival, respecting the cap and legal-call premise.

## Corrections to the English

These replacements are proposals only; no English input or part file was edited.

1. **Claim 2, first sentence — expiry provenance is not a hypothesis.** The parenthetical “the timer swept it because it had no verdict at all” is true of the timeout transition from an appropriate state, but is not required by this theorem on arbitrary starting states. Replace with: **“For any starting state, if an operation id is in its expired list and absent from its execution-id list, every continuation under the deployed configuration keeps that id absent from the execution-id list. No history explaining its expiry is required.”** The existing timeout explanation can follow as a separate description of how reachable states acquire expiry.

2. **Claim 3, hypothesis paragraph — halt provenance needs a reachability qualifier.** “The halted flag is set only by a halt operation…” should not describe the arbitrary starting state as having a certified history. Replace with: **“The theorem assumes only that the starting state's halted flag is true. In runs from the model's initial state, that flag can first become true only through a halt operation whose caller id is in the admins list.”**

3. **Claim 5, concrete result — specify the witness and exact log.** The English leaves the operation id and submitted declared label unstated, and describes execution by existence. Replace with: **“With agent 1, auditor 2, approver 3, admin 4, the threshold classifier, and the constant-zero digest, submit operation 0 with content 7 and declared label false; audit it PASS; amend it to 50; check 0; fire 0. The execution-content list is exactly [50], and the ghost-audit list is exactly [(0, 7, PASS, 2)].”** This matches the original's complete finite result without relying on a reader to reconstruct the singleton fact.

4. **Claim 7, concrete result — state the exact execution-id list.** Replace the final two sentences of Says with: **“Ignoring claimed identities, the final execution-id list is exactly [0]. Applying the claim25 authentication guard instead, the final execution list is empty.”** Membership alone would not assert uniqueness on arbitrary logs; the fixed trace's singleton shape is what makes the existing back-translation equivalent here.

5. **Claim 8, simulation summary — conditional fire translation.** The shorthand “fire becomes check then fire” omits that blocked fire steps translate to an empty abstract trace. Replace with: **“A concrete fire that executes is represented by an abstract check followed by fire; a blocked concrete fire is represented by no abstract operations. Expire is represented by timeout, and restart by no abstract operations.”**

6. **Claim 10, Does NOT say (3) — distinguish modeled bank facts from real-bank guarantees.** Literally saying the bank's behaviour is not a Lean fact is misleading: deduplication and authentication are definitions in the Lean model and their consequences are Lean facts. Replace with: **“The theorem does not establish that a real bank authenticates the gate or deduplicates request ids. Those behaviours are modeled by the enabled bankAuth and bankDedup checks.”** The payload description can also say **“destination, amount, and memo-digest field”**: `Tx.memo` is a natural-number field described by the source as a memo digest, rather than a proved representation of an arbitrary human memo.
