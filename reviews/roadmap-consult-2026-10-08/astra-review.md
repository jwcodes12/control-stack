The work has produced reusable formal components and useful engineering patterns. It has **not yet produced a reusable theorem connecting arbitrary deployed agents to those components**. The proposed roadmap should distinguish two tracks: the selected exact secret-recovery scenario, whose strongest guarantee is structural, and a future monitoring-based scenario, whose guarantee depends on empirical rates.

The strongest caveat is that **SC-03 peer confinement does not establish SC-01 confidentiality**. The egress fixture deliberately allows arbitrary CONNECT bytes and demonstrates secret delivery to an allowed peer ([egress/README.md:18](../../egress/README.md)). Its passing tests and Lean invariant support immediate-peer reachability. They do not bound what permitted services, downstream receivers, logs, or persistent memory can observe.

The eight-flow calibration is sufficient reason to proceed with the bounded integration experiment, but not to postpone the scenario contract. The existing follow-up already states the right immediate engineering experiment ([review:108](../../reviews/egress-follow-up-2026-10-08.md)).

Full Python-plus-Linux refinement should **not** be the immediate milestone. The best return is a small trusted transition/accounting core, explicit runtime-to-model correspondence obligations, differential and mutation tests, and clearly bounded residual assumptions. A full refinement project becomes justified after this boundary stabilizes.

A concrete lifetime-accounting obligation deserves attention now: `LifetimeLedger.charge` uses an unlocked read–modify–write followed by `os.replace`; missing state initializes fresh usage ([gateway.py:112](../../gateway/gateway.py)). The gateway and harness also permit `ledger=None`. Atomic replacement does not itself serialize admissions. This is a static finding about the deployment contract, not a demonstrated exploit in the current sequential fixture. Certified invocation needs enforced single-writer serialization or transactional accounting, durable state semantics, and trusted lineage assignment. Concurrency and restart therefore belong in the current milestone, not the later “broader stateful attacks” bucket.

The independent packet/syscall oracles, mutation controls, source/evidence hashes, permission-before-effect pattern, exact configuration accounting, and premise/evidence manifests are valuable **engineering patterns**. EgressGate and AuditDispatch are **scenario-specific model theorems**. A common runtime trace-refinement/assume-guarantee library remains proposed work; the catalog’s F1–F8 families should not be presented as implemented libraries.

The smallest credible portability test is to factor the shared invariant-preservation/gate skeleton used by EgressGate and AuditDispatch, then instantiate a restricted “no protected dispatch without prior trusted audit” fixture. Avoid claiming that the live auto_workflow environment satisfies this contract: its agent-editable worker can bypass the modeled dispatcher ([auditgate/FIDELITY.md:14](../../auditgate/FIDELITY.md)). Keep the observation-bound and probabilistic-checker APIs separate; they certify different properties.

**How empirical rates enter valid certificates**

For a monitor-mediated scenario, FNR determines bad-action escape probabilities; audit/defer FPR changes budget consumption, stopping, and honest completion. They must enter the **actual transition rows**, not a detached ROC report. Pre-action timing, correct labels, matched tasks/version/scaffold, held-out policy selection, dependence/clustering, and simultaneous threshold/condition coverage all matter. [DESIGN-A2:11](../../DESIGN-A2.md) gives a good construction, including the clipped uncertainty polytope and rational checking.

A family-wise confidence statement means that, under the sampling assumptions, the resulting bound covers the specified population model with the stated confidence. It does not establish universal attack coverage. Adaptive attack search is useful falsification; its pass rate is not automatically an IID estimate or a worst-case miss bound. Content-adaptive attackers require universal/stratified conditional coverage, enforced action restrictions, or an explicit restricted-population claim. The existing counterexample proves that population miss `1/1000` can coexist with adaptive catastrophe probability `1` ([ComposeBlindSpot:5](../../ControlStack/ComposeBlindSpot.lean)).

For the exact finite-view secret-recovery claim, sampled monitor FPR/FNR are unnecessary unless monitoring becomes part of the enforcement argument. Structural cardinality supplies the risk bound; empirical work should first establish useful operation and falsify missing channels.

Parallel work can cover outer-topology integration, lifetime/core correspondence, workload definition, and manifest/interface cleanup. Protocol specification and measurement design for the optional monitoring track can also proceed independently. Keep sharper mathematics last.
